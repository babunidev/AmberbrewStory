# Professions and skills

_Status: Established professions; exact skill levels undated._

## Current-life primary professions

- **Alchemy:** Her true present-life calling, with the opportunity to invent brews, potions and mixtures.
- **Herbalism:** Gathering and learning from the plants and ingredients her experiments require.

Her interest grows naturally out of the Amberbrew Brewery and the family's cooking and drink-making traditions.

## Other practiced skills

- **Cooking:** She is a passionate cook and chef, raised around the Amberbrew kitchen.
- **Fishing:** An enjoyable pursuit as well as a way of gathering ingredients and food.
- **First Aid:** Practical care for herself and others during adventures.

## Blacksmithing belongs to another life

Buxbrew **was a blacksmith in an actual earlier life/incarnation**. She did **not** start this lifetime as a blacksmith and then switch careers. Nor did she forge her current sword Blackguard: she commissioned it from a skilled weaponsmith.

## Unfixed details

Exact profession levels, recipe lists, specialization, training chronology and the mechanism of that previous incarnation have not been established.
