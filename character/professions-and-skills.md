# Professions and skills

_Status: Blacksmithing established as her current profession; exact skill level and training chronology undated._

## Current profession

- **Blacksmithing:** A craft that fascinated her throughout childhood. Growing up in Ironforge, she felt a strong connection to the Great Forge and the smiths working there. Only recently has she taken up the craft herself. She is particularly interested in making armor and useful equipment.

## Other practiced skills

- **Cooking:** She is a passionate cook and chef, raised around the Amberbrew kitchen.
- **Fishing:** An enjoyable pursuit.
- **First Aid:** Practical care for herself and others during adventures.

## Unfixed details

Her second profession, exact profession level, recipes, specialization, training chronology and the details of her early exposure to smithing have not been established.

Her sword **Blackguard** remains a separate established matter: she collected its materials and commissioned a skilled weaponsmith to make it. She did not forge that weapon herself.
