# Backstory

_Status: Established background and original family lore; exact dates unsettled._

## Family, birthplace and early life

Buxbrew's birth name is **Jasmine Amberbrew**. The Amberbrew Brewery and Tavern, concealed beneath the **Bronze Kettle** in Ironforge in the family's original setting, is her home and the center of her childhood. The household is shaped by kitchen work, brewing, food, guests, boisterous hospitality and a closely held Dark Iron heritage.

Her **mother Bess** is the brewery's welcoming, thunder-voiced presence. Her **father Arandor** is a paladin who is often away from home. Her **older sister Loni**, known as Lonebrew, is an independent rogue who values solitude. **Morkah**, Arandor's father and her paternal grandfather, figures centrally in the family's older history. Earlier hunter portrayals of Arandor and Loni have been superseded; no in-world class-change events have been established.

## The name Buxbrew

The nickname **Buxbrew** grew among brewery guests and friends of the Amberbrews, combining their joking appreciation of Jasmine's generous bosom with the family name. Some early accounts describe guests speaking of “that buxom lass and her wicked brew.” The name belongs to the people who call her by it: **she did not choose it for herself**, and her own name is Jasmine. Its first use remains undated.

## Family's earlier history

In **original Amberbrew fan lore**, Morkah broke with Thaurissan's destructive direction and led or preserved the family in concealment. Enchanted pendants let Amberbrews pass as Bronzebeard dwarves among people who would distrust Dark Irons. This heritage is described further in [Amberbrew lore](../lore/amberbrew-original-lore.md); it is not an official Warcraft historical claim.

## Fighting and calling

Jasmine has a protector's instinct, but at the beginning of her present adventure she fights as a close-range brawler, using fist weapons only. Her current journey has only just begun beyond Ironforge.

Blacksmithing was a strong childhood fascination. Growing up in Ironforge, she felt closely connected to the Great Forge and the craft practised there. She was very interested in the smiths and their work throughout her childhood. Only recently has she taken up blacksmithing herself, moving from a longstanding fascination to learning the craft with her own hands. She is particularly interested in making armor and useful equipment.

Cooking, fishing and first aid remain among her other established skills. The exact date she began smithing, her training history and her second profession have not been established.

A developed early-adventure account follows the hidden route out of Ironforge toward Dun Morogh, Thelsamar and Loch Modan, but its specific encounters must remain **narrative material** unless established as real gameplay or character history.
