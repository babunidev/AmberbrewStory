# Concept — A living Buxbrew archive

Buxbrew's story is larger than a character sheet. Through adventures, family lore, roleplaying and written scenes, her world can grow while remaining recognizable.

For this project, I want the established details to survive across sessions and new stories without being silently rewritten. The archive keeps character facts, recollections and fictional passages in different places, while leaving room for uncertain or unfinished ideas.

**Editorial principles**

- **Continuity:** Confirmed details are easy to find and contradictions stay visible until resolved.
- **Creative ownership:** The human creative decides what belongs in the character's history.
- **Living documentation:** Stories and character pages can evolve without pretending every draft is canon.
- **Traceable collaboration:** AI assistance is acknowledged without substituting for human experience or direction.
- **Readable storytelling:** The archive is meant to be explored by people, not only indexed by tools.

This is a fan-created project set in World of Warcraft, not an official Warcraft lore resource.
