# Development journal

A concise, public-facing development history told from the human creative's perspective. Creative choices and AI assistance remain distinguishable. Earlier entries are retained as records of stages that preceded the expanded family project.

## 2026-11-01 — Establishing the archive

With Buxbrew's stories and family history gathered into a dedicated project, I wanted a place where confirmed character facts could coexist with adventures and new fiction without losing their boundaries. The initial repository established separate areas for the character, her family, relationships, places, stories and a gameplay journal. AI assistance helped organize and revise the documentation; the creative choices and canon remain mine.

## 2026-11-01 — Making the structure useful

The first version already had the right categories, but many pages held empty headings despite details established in other discussions. During this documentation review, I directed AI assistance toward expanding those pages from the current canon, clarifying where memories differ from fiction and adding concise records for decisions and collaboration. The sisters' birth-order question was kept open pending an explicit decision.

## 2026-11-01 — Bringing the Amberbrews home

After the repository audit, I settled the old contradiction: **Loni, known as Lonebrew, is Buxbrew's older sister**, and Buxbrew is the younger one. Her given name is Loni Amberbrew. Another missing relative was **Arandor Amberbrew**, Buxbrew's father, a hunter who is often away somewhere on his travels; **Morkah remains the grandfather**. Using AI assistance, the archive was then expanded with previously established details from our character conversations, while keeping dramatized events and undated anecdotes separate from proven gameplay. The family pages, canon, chronology and continuity notes were updated to agree.

## 2026-11-01 — From Buxbrew to Amberbrew Story

As the family's place in the project became clearer, the single-character name felt too narrow. During a discussion about how these stories should be approached, I decided to make the **whole Amberbrew family** the subject, with Buxbrew still leading the first stage. The repository became **AmberbrewStory**, reflecting that broader direction without discarding the earlier character archive.

## 2026-11-01 — Defining the feeling, then documenting it

Ten questions helped clarify what I wanted future Amberbrew writing to preserve: grounded language with a little poetry, characters discovered through lived moments, a warm and imperfect family, natural humor, unhurried pacing and mysteries that can remain unexplained. Playing Buxbrew will bring the adventures; attention to everyday life will give the family room to exist beyond them. AI asked the questions and drafted a synthesis of the answers, then helped organize the confirmed choices in the project's concept, writing guide, decisions and roadmap. This was **documentation work, not the writing of new stories**.

## 2026-11-01 — Writing the stories after documenting the rules

With the family archive and writing principles in place, I asked for the Amberbrew stories discussed in earlier conversations to be gathered and rewritten for the repository. The first adventure gave us a recognizable path through Dun Morogh and Thelsamar toward Loch Modan, while the rest of the family deserved quieter pages of their own. AI reconstructed seven literary scenes from recovered discussion summaries and the existing canon pages, including invented dialogue and small domestic details. Their notes distinguish source-based events from dramatization; none was entered as verified gameplay or silently adopted as permanent canon.

## 2026-11-01 — Finding the family's voice on the page

Reading the first group of stories, I wanted the narration to stay closer to Jasmine's experience without repeatedly calling her by the nickname other people gave her. The family's guests and friends may call her Buxbrew, but the prose can usually simply say she. More of the daily movement, textures and little events belong between the spoken lines, too. After I clarified that approach and the spelling of Arandor's name, AI revised the seven chapters and corrected the family references across the repository.

## 2026-11-05 — Loni's new character direction

With a new in-game character sheet for Loni, I changed her class fantasy from hunter to rogue. Her appearance now has short dark hair, and the earlier petless-hunter framing no longer fits. Rather than explain the change through an invented incident, I asked for the stories and family records to be brought into agreement. AI reviewed the repository and revised her profile, linked canon references and the scenes that depended on the old portrayal. The established bond between the sisters and Loni's quieter nature remain intact; her rogue specialization and personal history are still open.

## 2026-11-05 — Updating the family's present-day identities

With the latest character sheets, my family archive needed another continuity pass: Arandor is now a paladin, Loni is a rogue, and Morkah is Arandor's father. Earlier hunter descriptions no longer represent their current identities. At my request, AI reconciled reference pages and the two affected domestic drafts, preserving the characters' relationships and the stories' invented-scene status. Neither character has been given an unconfirmed class-change event or affiliation.

## 2026-11-07 — Bringing Jasmine back to the forge

The profession notes no longer reflected how I wanted Jasmine to feel in the present life. Growing up in Ironforge, she had always been very interested in blacksmithing and the Great Forge; recently, she has begun practising the craft herself. I chose to replace the former Alchemy and Herbalism direction and remove the earlier-incarnation explanation for smithing. AI reviewed the connected character records, revised the canon, timeline, profession notes and related documentation, and adjusted two domestic story drafts whose scenes had relied on her former herbal work. Blackguard remains a weapon she commissioned from a skilled weaponsmith, and her second profession is still undecided.
