# Buxbrew — Character timeline

Only established ordering is recorded here. Uncertain dates, narrative reconstructions and gameplay milestones are explicitly marked.

## Amberbrew family history — historical background, exact dates unset

- In the family's **original fan lore**, Morkah rejected the path followed by Thaurissan and his followers and helped separate the Amberbrews from it.
- The Amberbrews preserved their lineage and concealed their home and brewery in Ironforge, using magical disguises.
- **Morkah is Arandor's father**, establishing the paternal line to Jasmine and Loni.
- These events describe **family history**, not necessarily episodes witnessed by Buxbrew.

## Buxbrew's present-life upbringing

- Born Jasmine Amberbrew, she grows up in the orbit of the Amberbrew family, its brewery, kitchen, guests and traditions.
- Growing up in Ironforge, she develops a strong childhood fascination with blacksmithing and the Great Forge.
- Only recently, in her present life, does she take up blacksmithing herself. The exact date and details of her training are not established.
- She is **younger than her sister Loni (“Lonebrew”) Amberbrew**.
- Her mother is Bess “Bosom Bess” Amberbrew; her father, Arandor, is often absent on his travels; his current class is paladin. Her older sister Loni is now a rogue. Neither class change has a confirmed in-world date or explanation.
- Patrons' description of her as “that buxom lass and her wicked brew” develops into the name **Buxbrew**. The exact moment is not dated.

## Her present beginning as an adventurer

- In early adulthood, Jasmine has only recently begun adventuring beyond Ironforge and has started a fresh leveling journey.
- Her fighting style is that of a brawler: she uses **fist weapons only**.
- She has a protective instinct, but her current approach is direct and physical rather than a developed tanking style.
- She has recently taken up **Blacksmithing**, a craft that fascinated her throughout childhood. Cooking, Fishing and First Aid remain among her other established skills.
- In **Loch Modan**, while sitting beside a lakeside fire and staring into the flames, Jasmine first experiences **Eldgrim** as a warm, friendly and welcoming voice in her mind. It encourages her to stay with and keep the fire and to find others drawn to the same warmth and companionship.
- Following that encounter, she begins seeking other adventurers who share those values. She later gathers signatures in **Kharanos** and founds **Amberhearth**. Eldgrim initiates the fellowship's idea but remains outside its conventional ranks; **ROAR** is the wider community context.
- During the current period she is largely on her own, adventuring and finding people for Amberhearth. She has not been in contact with her family for some time because she has been absorbed in the road and the new fellowship; no family conflict is established by that absence.

Those later-stage details have been removed from Jasmine's active continuity following the creative's reset of her adventure to its early beginning. They should not be reintroduced unless the creative establishes them again.

- Loni is now a **rogue**, superseding her earlier hunter portrayal. Neither change is assigned an invented in-world date or origin.
