# Buxbrew — Established canon

This is the index of facts established by the human creative. The topic pages expand these facts; invented scenes, possible interpretations and gameplay recollections must retain their own labels.

## Identity

- **Primary name:** Buxbrew. Use this name in narration and character references.
- **Birth name:** Jasmine Amberbrew.
- **Race:** Female Dark Iron dwarf.
- **Family:** Amberbrew, a Dark Iron family with a concealed history in Ironforge.
- **Age:** Described as a young adult, roughly 24 in human-equivalent years; no birth date is set.
- **Nickname:** Tavern patrons' description of “that buxom lass and her wicked brew” was shortened to **Buxbrew**.

## Family

- **Mother:** Bess Amberbrew, affectionately called **Bosom Bess**, a warm, booming-voiced center of the family brewery.
- **Father:** **Erendor Amberbrew**, a hunter who is frequently away, somewhere out in the world.
- **Older sister:** **Loni Amberbrew**, known as **Lonebrew**, an independent hunter who deliberately does not use a pet.
- **Younger sister:** **Buxbrew (Jasmine Amberbrew)**. Buxbrew is younger than Loni; the earlier contrary description is superseded.
- **Grandfather:** **Morkah Amberbrew** (also transcribed “Murka” in conversation), an Amberbrew elder associated with the family's break from Thaurissan's course. His precise maternal/paternal line is unspecified.
- No other relatives or marriages are established merely by this summary.

See [the family](family/amberbrew-family.md), [Erendor](family/erendor-amberbrew.md) and [Loni](family/lonebrew.md).

## Home, clan and affiliations

- **Home:** Amberbrew Brewery and Tavern, concealed beneath the **Bronze Kettle** in Ironforge in the original family lore.
- The Amberbrews separated from the Dark Iron course that led toward Thaurissan's domination and Ragnaros; Morkah's role and the clan's concealment belong to **original fan lore**, not official Warcraft history.
- The family uses enchanted pendants to appear like Bronzebeard dwarves to most observers; the disguise does not change their stature and may be seen through.
- **Guild:** Roar (also styled ROAR). Buxbrew has been described as its founder and adventures with its members.

## Appearance

- Stout, thickset, physically powerful Dark Iron dwarf; strongly built rather than delicate.
- Wild **auburn/copper-red hair**, often braided with **copper beads**.
- **Deep amber eyes**, ruddy cheeks and expressive bearing.
- No definitive piercing/earring design has been confirmed. Written references and art should not invent one as a fact.

## Personality and mannerisms

- Cheerful, exuberant, loud, welcoming, stubborn, generous and fiercely protective.
- Loves family, food, brewing, celebration and laughter; dances on tavern tables.
- Previously described **Rhapsody Malt** as a favorite drink and **beer-basted boar ribs** as a much-loved meal after fights. These are personal preferences, not confirmed proprietary Amberbrew recipes.
- Bows, roars, laughs, sometimes howls or beats her chest; greets others with **“Greetings!”** and **“Cheers!”**
- Forgiving and practical about mistakes: mishaps happen on adventures, and solutions are found.
- Fights with joy and energy rather than being defined by tragedy.

## Professions and abilities

- **Current primary professions:** Alchemy and Herbalism.
- **Additional skills:** Cooking, Fishing and First Aid.
- Cooking and brewing are rooted in family upbringing; experimentation with herbs and recipes suits her.
- **Blacksmithing was practiced in an earlier life/incarnation**, never as a previous career in her present life.

## Combat and equipment

- **Role:** Protector, warrior and tank; she has always sought to protect people.
- **Current style:** Plate armor, sword and shield; more disciplined than her earlier brawling style.
- **Weapon:** Blackguard, made by a skilled weaponsmith from materials Buxbrew collected after hearing of the weapon through the Thorium Brotherhood.
- **Shield:** A personally made, spiked shield in established character accounts.
- **Other character equipment:** Battlegear of Heroism (as described in earlier narrative references); Lion Horn drinking horn.
- **Mount:** Armored brown boar.
- At one documented point, she was pleased with her largely epic equipment, saving for weapon-skill training and searching for rings and a trinket. These are **time-bound goals**, not permanent equipment status.

## Motto

> “We roar, we laugh, we fight.”

## Boundaries

- No exact present-life birth date, definitive childhood chronology, full genealogy or precise sequence of all adventures has been established.
- Specific adventure scenes (including an early Dun Morogh/Loch Modan account) can be preserved as **narrative accounts** without claiming every incident happened in gameplay.
- Do not treat the Amberbrew family lore as official Blizzard canon.
- Details from new conversations are added only when the creative establishes them; earlier conflicting sister-order wording is no longer an open question.
