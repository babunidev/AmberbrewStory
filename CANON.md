# Buxbrew — Established Canon

This document holds confirmed character facts.

## Identity
- Primary name: Buxbrew
- Full name: Jasmine Amberbrew
- Race: Dark Iron dwarf
- Family: Amberbrew

## Family
- Mother: Bess Amberbrew, known as Bosom Bess
- Grandfather: Morkah
- Sister: Lonebrew

## Home and affiliations
- Family home: Amberbrew Brewery
- Strong connection to Ironforge
- Guild: Roar

## Personality
- Cheerful, boisterous and protective
- Deeply devoted to family and friends
- Enjoys laughter, celebration and hospitality
- Accepts mistakes as part of adventuring

## Professions
- Primary: Alchemy and Herbalism
- Additional: Cooking, Fishing and First Aid
- Blacksmithing belongs to an earlier
  life/incarnation, not her current career

## Combat
- Protector and tank
- Plate armor, sword and shield
- Known weapon: Blackguard
- Earlier fighting style was more brawling-oriented

## Mount
- Armored brown boar

## Motto
"We roar, we laugh, we fight."

## Unresolved continuity
- Earlier descriptions call Buxbrew the youngest
  Amberbrew, while Lonebrew is described as her
  younger sister. The exact sibling birth order
  must remain unresolved until clarified.

## Canon rules
- Confirmed facts may be expanded, not silently changed.
- New interpretations must be marked as proposals.
- Contradictions must be documented.
- Gameplay events and fictional scenes must be
  distinguished when their factual status matters.
