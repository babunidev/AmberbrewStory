# Buxbrew — The Story of Jasmine Amberbrew

A living, fan-created character archive for **Buxbrew** (Jasmine Amberbrew), a Dark Iron dwarf in the World of Warcraft setting. Her character, the Amberbrew family lore and the stories in this repository are under the human creative's direction.

The archive distinguishes **established canon**, **recorded adventures**, **fictional narratives**, **unconfirmed ideas**, and **the documented process of developing this project**. An empty topic page is not an invitation to invent missing facts.

> **Motto:** “We roar, we laugh, we fight.”

## Status and provenance

**Established:** confirmed by the human creative and recorded in [CANON.md](CANON.md).
**Draft:** incomplete prose or documentation; it does not establish new facts.
**Proposed:** an idea awaiting a creative decision.
**Unresolved:** conflicting or missing information, preserved without guessing.

The known contradiction about the order of the Amberbrew siblings remains [unresolved](lore/continuity-notes.md).
