# Buxbrew — The Story of Jasmine Amberbrew

A living character archive dedicated to Buxbrew,
a Dark Iron dwarf of the Amberbrew family,
living within the World of Warcraft universe.

This repository collects her identity, history,
family, relationships, adventures and stories.

## Purpose

The archive is intended to grow over time as
Buxbrew develops through gameplay, roleplaying,
storytelling and worldbuilding.

Established facts remain separate from
interpretations, ideas and fictional embellishments.
