# Stories

Written stories and chapters about Buxbrew belong here. Individual narratives go in [chapters/](chapters/); begin with [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md).

**A published story is not automatically a confirmed gameplay memory or an update to character canon.** Mark whether its events are fiction, a dramatization of a journal entry or specifically approved canon. Keep unresolved continuity questions visible and cross-link source material when applicable.

For voice and authorship guidance see [WRITING-GUIDE.md](../WRITING-GUIDE.md).
