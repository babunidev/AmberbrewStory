# Amberbrew stories

The chapters here are **AI-assisted narrative rewrites and new scenes grounded in the human creative's established Amberbrew lore and earlier character conversations**. They are not transcripts or verified World of Warcraft gameplay logs. Buxbrew is the initial central figure, but the stories belong to the whole family.

Read the fiction itself as fiction. At the end of each chapter, a clearly separated continuity note identifies the established anchors and the details invented for that telling. An invented conversation, visitor, object, route or moment is not automatically permanent canon.

## The first journey — connected narrative reconstruction

Read these in order. Their underlying incidents were described in earlier character conversations, but their wording, scene transitions and most dialogue have been newly composed.

1. [Out into the Snow](chapters/02-out-into-the-snow.md) — from the hidden Ironforge passages through Dun Morogh, the airfield country and encounters with a winter wolf and Frostmane trolls.
2. [The Stoutlager Inn](chapters/03-the-stoutlager-inn.md) — a meal in Thelsamar, an overturned mug, a brief tavern scuffle and a long-awaited supper.
3. [Before the Sun Reached the Loch](chapters/04-before-the-sun-reached-the-loch.md) — an island, troggs and the return toward shore at dawn.
