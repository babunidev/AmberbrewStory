## Resolved — sibling birth order (2026-11-01)

**Former conflict:** Some early descriptions called Buxbrew the youngest Amberbrew, while others called Lonebrew her younger sister.

**Creative's definitive decision:** **Loni “Lonebrew” Amberbrew is the older sister. Jasmine “Buxbrew” Amberbrew is the younger sister.**

**Status:** **RESOLVED.** Earlier descriptions of Lonebrew as younger are superseded. They are preserved here solely to explain why older drafts disagree; no longer flag the order as open in canon or topic pages.

**Related identity clarification:** Lonebrew's given name is **Loni Amberbrew**. **Arandor Amberbrew** is Buxbrew's father and a paladin who is often away. **Morkah (sometimes transcribed “Murka”)** is Arandor's father and therefore her paternal grandfather.

## Resolved — Loni's rogue identity (2026-11-05)

**Current canon:** Loni Amberbrew is a **rogue**, not a hunter. All earlier petless-hunter references are superseded. Her father Arandor is now a paladin.

The new in-game character reference shows Loni with **short charcoal-dark hair** and **golden-amber eyes**, replacing the older amber-hair description. Her reserved, independent temperament and closeness with Jasmine remain established. Her rogue specialization, training and whether hunting ever formed part of her in-world history have **not** been confirmed. This class-fantasy update is not a dated in-world event.

**Status:** Settled. Buxbrew's blacksmithing was in an actual **earlier life/incarnation**, not an earlier occupation in her current lifetime. Blackguard was commissioned from a skilled weaponsmith.

## Established — narrative naming

**Jasmine Amberbrew** is her personal name; **Buxbrew** is a familiar nickname given by brewery guests and family friends in reference to her generous bosom and family surname. It is not her self-selected name or the preferred name of the narrative voice. Literary narration should favor she/her once she is established, with Jasmine used sparingly for clarity. Her father's name is **Arandor Amberbrew**.
