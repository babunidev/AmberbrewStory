# The Amberbrew family

_Status: Established known relationships; wider genealogy undetermined._

The Amberbrews are a **Dark Iron** family whose original lore places a concealed **brewery and tavern beneath the Bronze Kettle in Ironforge**. Their household prizes **brewing, cooking, hospitality, laughter, close kinship and the restrained use of power**.

## Known family members

| Person | Relationship and established role |
| --- | --- |
| **[Bess “Bosom Bess” Amberbrew](bess-amberbrew.md)** | Buxbrew's mother; welcoming, booming-voiced brewery hostess and household presence |
| **[Erendor Amberbrew](erendor-amberbrew.md)** | Buxbrew's father; **hunter**, habitually away somewhere on his travels |
| **[Loni “Lonebrew” Amberbrew](lonebrew.md)** | **Older sister**; solitary hunter who fights without a pet |
| **[Jasmine “Buxbrew” Amberbrew](../character/overview.md)** | **Younger sister**; brewer-hearted warrior, alchemist and protector |
| **[Morkah Amberbrew](morkah.md)** | Buxbrew's **grandfather** and an elder of the clan's history; “Murka” is a variant transcription |

**Confirmed sibling order:** **Loni is older than Buxbrew.** Previous descriptions that made Lonebrew younger are superseded. The family may contain relatives not yet documented; “younger sister” does not assert a complete census of children.

## The family as a household

The sisters contrast. **Loni** finds contentment alone in forests and mountains; **Buxbrew** thrives around the hearth and among others. Both are capable hunters and warriors of their own kinds, and a visit with enough drink can turn the shy older sister into Buxbrew's loud partner in mischief. **Erendor** shares the hunting life, but no specific reason has been established for his frequent absences. **Bess** is the welcoming center of home.

## Original Dark Iron lineage

In fan-created Amberbrew history, Morkah chose separation rather than submission to Thaurissan's destructive course. The family employs a magical disguise to pass among other dwarves in Ironforge.
