# The Amberbrew family

_Status: Partially established._

## Confirmed relationships

- **Buxbrew (Jasmine Amberbrew):** Dark Iron dwarf and member of the family.
- **Bess Amberbrew:** Buxbrew's mother; also known as **Bosom Bess**.
- **Morkah:** Buxbrew's grandfather.
- **Lonebrew:** Buxbrew's sister.

The **Amberbrew Brewery** is the family's home. Brewing, cooking, hospitality and family traditions are part of Buxbrew's upbringing.

## Unresolved

The family tree is incomplete. Buxbrew has been described both as the youngest Amberbrew and as having a younger sister, Lonebrew; neither sibling ordering has been resolved.

See [continuity notes](../lore/continuity-notes.md), [Bess](bess-amberbrew.md), [Morkah](morkah.md) and [Lonebrew](lonebrew.md).
