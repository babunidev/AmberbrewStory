# Bess “Bosom Bess” Amberbrew

_Status: Established family portrayal; exact age and chronology unknown._

**Bess Amberbrew** is **Buxbrew's mother** and a cherished presence at the Amberbrew Brewery and Tavern. Her affectionate nickname, **Bosom Bess**, is playful rather than a rank or title.

## Character and hospitality

Bess is **cheerful, warm, welcoming and quick to laugh**. Her great, roaring voice can fill the room even before she appears. She thrives amid guests, family, cooking, brews and good-natured tavern chaos. To her, a home is better when people feel free to relax, celebrate and be themselves.

Her generosity does not make her helpless: she runs her surroundings with practiced confidence, and her playful tolerance for drunken antics is **her choice**, not an absence of boundaries.

## Physical presence

She is portrayed as a **voluptuous, powerfully built Dark Iron dwarf**, with generous curves, famously strong thighs, a pronounced bosom and an expressive, formidable presence. Her **booming voice and laughter** are at least as memorable as her appearance.

## Relationship to Buxbrew

Bess's warmth, hospitality and exuberance resonate in Buxbrew, though each is her own person. Bess is established as **Buxbrew's mother**; the precise chronology of the parents' relationship is not documented.
