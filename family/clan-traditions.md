# Amberbrew family traditions

_Status: Established household values and original-lore customs; no fixed rituals or recipes._

## Hearth, food and drink

Buxbrew grows up around the **brewery, kitchens and tavern hospitality**. Brewing, cooking and a generous welcome are part of ordinary family life. There is warmth and considerable noise where Bess holds court; Buxbrew inherited a taste for laughter, entertaining and experimenting with ingredients.

## Dark Iron identity and concealment

The Amberbrews retain their **Dark Iron heritage** despite dwelling in Ironforge. Their **enchanted pendants** allow them to appear as **Bronzebeard dwarves** without changing their physical proportions. The spell is dependable, not something that slips when the wearer drinks, fights, falls, catches the chain or suffers ordinary mishaps.

**Keeping the family's true heritage hidden is one of its most important rules and traditions.** No member exposes it to Bronzebeard society, whether out of bravado, anger, carelessness or convenience. Their safety, home and life among their neighbors depend on that discretion. No person outside the family may casually penetrate the disguise for narrative tension. The human creative alone may authorize any change to this rule.

## Power with restraint

Morkah's history gives the family a theme of **controlled fire, strength and shadow**, chosen rather than uncontrolled surrender to dangerous power. This is original roleplay lore and must not be promoted to general Warcraft canon.

## What is not defined

No exact named beer recipe, formal initiation, strict clan code, holiday ritual or hereditary title is established just because the family brews. Such traditions require an explicit creative decision.
