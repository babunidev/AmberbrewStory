# Loni “Lonebrew” Amberbrew

_Status: Established sister profile; chronology and age beyond birth order unconfirmed._

## Identity and place in the family

**Loni Amberbrew**, known as **Lonebrew**, is a **Dark Iron dwarf** and the **older sister** of **Jasmine “Buxbrew” Amberbrew**. The creative resolved their order on 2026-11-01: **Loni is older; Buxbrew is younger.**

## Hunter without a pet

Loni is a **hunter who deliberately fights without an animal companion**. Her independence is intentional: she trusts her own ability and prefers not to depend on a pet.

## Temperament and appearance

She is **shy, introverted, quiet and self-reliant**, happiest alone in wilderness, forests and mountains rather than in noisy towns. Her established appearance includes **short amber hair**, a **healthy complexion** and a **round, generously built backside**. The latter is a character-design description, not a statement about her abilities.

## Loni and Buxbrew

The sisters represent **wilderness and hearth**. Buxbrew thrives among guests, brewing, friends and boisterous fighting; Loni seeks peace away from crowds. Nevertheless, when Loni visits Buxbrew and the two drink together, Loni's reserve drops and **both become loud, mischievous and prone to drunken troublemaking**.

## Other family ties

Their father **Arandor** is also a hunter, often away from home. No specific hunting partnership between father and daughter has been established. Their mother is **Bess**, and their grandfather is **Morkah**.
