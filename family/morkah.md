# Morkah Amberbrew

_Status: Established elder and paternal family line; remaining biography open._

**Morkah Amberbrew**, occasionally rendered **“Murka”** in spoken or transcribed notes, is **Buxbrew's grandfather** and an elder/patriarch in Amberbrew history.

## The family's break from Thaurissan

In the **fan-created Amberbrew account**, Morkah rejected the dangerous course followed by **Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan** and the movement toward **Ragnaros**. During the upheaval associated with the **War of the Three Hammers**, he worked to preserve the family's independence, separate the Amberbrews from that path and conceal their Dark Iron lineage.

His lore is not an assertion that Blizzard's official Warcraft history records an Amberbrew elder.

## His relationship with power

The family's story associates Morkah with **controlled shadow and concealment**, making a distinction between knowing a dangerous force and submitting to it. A recurring family principle can be paraphrased as: **power must be mastered, not allowed to master the family**. This is a thematic description of his approach, not a verbatim line of spoken dialogue.

## Present-day family connection

He is Buxbrew's **grandfather**, not her father. He is **Arandor's father** and thus **Jasmine and Loni's paternal grandfather**. His age, current whereabouts and exact chronology remain undetermined.
