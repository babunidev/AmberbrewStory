# Instructions for AI collaborators — Amberbrew Story

This is a **World of Warcraft family-story and worldbuilding archive**, not a software project. The human is the **creative**, author and creative director. AI supports the work without claiming those creative decisions.

## Active scope: family storytelling and careful documentation

The creative explicitly requested story rewriting on 2026-11-01 after the first documentation-only phase. **Write fiction only when requested**; put it under `stories/chapters/` and keep its invented dialogue, incidents and interpretations distinct from verified gameplay and established canon. Continue maintaining documentation and continuity records alongside authorized fiction.

The project is **Amberbrew Story** (repository: AmberbrewStory), formerly BuxbrewCharacterStory. It follows the **entire Amberbrew family**, with Buxbrew as initial central character.

## Established continuity anchors

- **Buxbrew** is Jasmine Amberbrew's primary character name.
- **Loni “Lonebrew” Amberbrew is older; Buxbrew is younger.**
- **Arandor Amberbrew** is their father, a traveling hunter; **Morkah** is the grandfather.
- Blacksmithing belongs to Buxbrew's **earlier incarnation**, not an occupation in the present life.
- Buxbrew commissioned Blackguard from a weaponsmith; she did not forge it herself.
- The family history and hidden tavern are **fan-created lore**, not official Blizzard history.

## Mandatory Amberbrew secrecy safeguard

The family **must remain unrecognized as Dark Iron by Bronzebeard dwarves**. Their disguise pendants are durable, dependable family magic, not a vulnerability to exploit for tension: casual contact, brawls, lost balance, snagged clothing or damage to a chain cannot make the illusion fail. Never disclose or imply disclosure through an invented outsider's detection, confession, or convenient accident. If a proposed plot depends on that outcome, change the plot. No exception without the human creative's explicit new direction.

## Narrative naming and scene composition

**In fiction, use she/her for Jasmine wherever the referent is clear; introduce or reintroduce her as Jasmine only when useful.** "Buxbrew" is an informal nickname used **by other characters**, especially guests and family friends. She did not name herself that, and the narrator must not repeatedly call her by it. The other family members are normally named Bess, Loni, Arandor and Morkah when clarity calls for a name. Do not invent additional nickname origins or switch Loni's name on the basis of a transcription ambiguity.

Favor **observed action, surroundings, sensory context and the ordinary passage of time** over constant dialogue. Dialogue remains welcome when it reveals a relationship or belongs naturally to a scene; there is no fixed dialogue quota. The protagonist need not be announced by name in every paragraph.

## Literary principles (for authorized story tasks)

**The creative has explicitly authorized a story-writing pass on 2026-11-01.** Draft fiction into `stories/chapters/`, label its relationship to canon, and keep invented dialogue/incidents separate from source-confirmed facts. The earlier documentation-only phase is historical, not a standing prohibition.

- Observant, discreet narration with private thoughts used sparingly
- Grounded, gently lyrical language with specific sensory details
- Actual gameplay adventures enriched by ordinary family life
- Believable connective inventions that never override established facts
- A good-hearted, supportive, imperfect family with low dramatic intensity
- Natural humor without forced jokes or spectacular embarrassment
- Unhurried pacing, with room for quiet gestures
- Mysteries permitted to remain unexplained indefinitely
- Warcraft lore respected as a foundation; World of Warcraft Forever may evolve
- Reader experience of **curious wonder**, as a welcomed passerby glimpsing an ongoing life

## Attribution and writing voice

Write public-facing project history **from the human creative's first-person perspective**. Use “I” and “my” naturally within sentences but **never start a sentence with “I” or “My”**. Use **creative**, never “creator”, as the role label.

Describe human direction and AI assistance without confusing them. Do not invent quotes, motivations, emotions, player achievements, dates, approval or implementation.

## Verification and updates

- Treat explicit user corrections and decisions as authoritative; assistant examples are **not** canon.
- Keep established lore, dramatization, gameplay and proposals separate.
- Record contradictions in lore/continuity-notes.md rather than silently choosing an answer.
- Update CANON.md and TIMELINE.md only for confirmed in-world facts or supported chronology.
- For substantive documentation work, update docs/DECISIONS.md and docs/DEVLOG.md as appropriate and CHANGELOG.md after actual edits.
- Keep links relative and valid; preserve unrelated history and avoid duplicating long passages.
- Never claim that a repository change, test or external verification happened until it actually did.
