# Instructions for AI Collaborators

This is a World of Warcraft character-story repository,
not a software development project.

## Before editing
Read:
1. README.md
2. CANON.md
3. TIMELINE.md
4. WRITING-GUIDE.md
5. The relevant topic files

## Rules
- Preserve established canon.
- Use Buxbrew as the character's primary name.
- Never silently invent biographical facts.
- Keep factual character records distinct from fiction.
- Do not confuse an earlier incarnation with a
  career change during the current lifetime.
- Record contradictions in lore/continuity-notes.md.
- Update TIMELINE.md when confirmed events are added.
- Update CANON.md when new facts are established.
- Update CHANGELOG.md for meaningful lore changes.
- Prefer expanding existing pages over duplicating facts.
- Keep each Markdown document focused on its own topic.
- Keep stories readable, vivid and character-driven.
- Do not present AI-generated suggestions as player history.

## Working across chats
Treat the repository as the persistent source of truth.
Previous conversation summaries may provide research
material but do not automatically override repository canon.

## Narration
In-world stories may use first-person or third-person
narration as appropriate. Out-of-character author notes
should reflect the human author's perspective.
