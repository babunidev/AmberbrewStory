# Amberbrew Brewery and Tavern

_Status: Established family location in original fan lore; architectural details undefined._

The **Amberbrew Brewery and Tavern** is Buxbrew's family home, hidden **beneath the Bronze Kettle in Ironforge** in the original Amberbrew setting. Its concealment fits the family's Dark Iron history and their desire to live apart from the course of Thaurissan's followers.

The place is a **working brewery, kitchen, gathering place and family hearth**, where food, drink, conversation and rowdy celebrations are welcome. **Bess “Bosom Bess”** brings a warm, extraordinary presence and a great roaring laugh. **Buxbrew** grows up surrounded by recipes, guests and family activity; **Loni** feels most at home away in the wilderness; **Arandor**, now a paladin, is frequently away on his travels. **Morkah's** decisions frame the family's older history.

## Concealment

The family's **pendant disguises** make it easier for Dark Iron Amberbrews to move among dwarves in Ironforge, and the protection is not casually defeated by spectators, arguments or ordinary mishaps. The family maintains its hidden identity without public disclosure. Exact access tunnels, entrances, room arrangement and capacity are not established as a fixed plan.
