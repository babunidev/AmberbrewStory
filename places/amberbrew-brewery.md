# Amberbrew Brewery

_Status: Partially established._

The **Amberbrew Brewery** is the Amberbrew family's home and a significant part of Buxbrew's upbringing. Brewing, cooking, hospitality and shared family life are central to its place in her history.

The archive does not yet establish a precise in-game address, floor plan, ownership history or dated events at the brewery. Scenes written there are narrative material unless independently confirmed as canon.
